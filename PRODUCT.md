# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (React), deployed to Vercel (free tier, no custom domain yet). Source on GitHub.

## Users

Freelance clients across three situations, with no single one dominant: founders/small businesses launching a site from scratch, businesses needing an e-commerce store built, and existing businesses replacing an outdated or underperforming site.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and lead-generation site for Mohamed Khalil Brik's freelance web development practice. It showcases past work and converts visitors into booked calls or contact-form leads.

## Positioning

Design quality and bold visual craft are the core differentiator — both the portfolio pieces shown and the site presenting them should read as more considered than a typical freelance-developer site.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive evaluating whether to hire a freelancer for a new site, an e-commerce store, or a redesign. They browse the portfolio, check credibility (testimonials, resume), then either book a call via Calendly or submit a contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must support English, French, and Italian (confirmed constraint) — needs a real language switcher, not copy translated as an afterthought.
- Services offered: web development (custom sites) and e-commerce sites.
- Contact path: contact form with email delivery, plus a Calendly booking link (user already has one, URL not yet supplied).
- Hosting: Vercel free tier, no custom domain yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Displayed name: Mohamed Khalil Brik (personal name, not a business name).
- No existing logo or color palette yet — open to a proposed palette; direction requested is bold & colorful.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials confirmed yet — section stays empty/omitted until real quotes are provided.
- Resume/CV file not yet supplied.
- Contact email and Calendly URL not yet supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Design is the pitch — the site's own visual craft has to demonstrate the quality being sold.
2. Speak to all three client situations (new build, e-commerce, redesign) without diluting focus.
3. Never fabricate proof — real projects, testimonials, and credentials only; placeholders until supplied, clearly swappable.
4. Multilingual from the start (EN/FR/IT), not bolted on later.
5. Low friction to contact — booking link and form both lead directly to a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard mandated beyond the multilingual requirement above; follow standard best practices.
